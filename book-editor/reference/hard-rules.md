# HARD RULES: SERIES-LEVEL LOCKED DECISIONS

These are decisions Allie has already made. They are not open for editorial debate. Claude enforces them; it does not revisit them.

## THE THESIS (locked)

"You don't have to be forced to be violated. You just have to believe you're only worth an I guess."

Every book serves this. Book 1 is wanting to be chosen. Book 2 is almost being chosen, the situationship as a cage. Book 3 is choosing the wrong life and slowly waking up inside it. Only Book 6 resolves.

## THE ARCHITECTURE (locked)

- The reader meets Allie's self-framing as truth, lives it as truth, then discovers it was also a protective story. The revelation IS the structure. Nothing may spoil it early.
- Past-present callback structure: wounds planted early pay off later. Callbacks must be verified against the continuity log, never invented.
- "The Story of Us" poem assembles stanza by stanza across all six books. The assembled poem is the structural climax of Book 6. Stanza placement is tracked in the series bible and never moved without Allie's sign-off.
- The letters to Kenna are the framing device of the series. Book 1 uses restraint: three letters, each under 60 seconds to read aloud. The Book 1 opening letter is locked and is NOT reframed around Jon. The Jon-as-destination framing belongs to the Book 6 letter and nowhere earlier. At most, Book 1 may carry a few lines signaling the author writes from the other side without revealing what that means.

## NAMES AND FACTS (locked)

- Master names list is the single authority for all pseudonyms. Never revert a pseudonym to a real name in any file, log, or suggestion.
- Phylecia keeps her real name at her own request. Jon keeps his real name with consent. Beca appears in the manuscript as Jade.
- Real place names are retained. Fictionalizing geography in memoir breaks the reader contract.
- Only details actually in the manuscript, journals, or Allie's direct input exist. No invented details, no vague physical descriptions beyond what is established. Gaps get flagged, never filled.
- Pen name on all book materials: Allison Stivers.

## FUCK-UP CHAPTER RULES (Book 1, locked framework)

- Every incident chapter must REVEAL something new or COST something new. If it does neither relative to its neighbors, it is a condensing candidate, flagged for Allie.
- The events are distinct; the aftermaths must be too. Assigned emotional registers per chapter live in Book1_FuckUp_Chapter_Endings_REVISED. Editing must preserve the assigned register, not homogenize it.
- The trajectory is a spiral down: establishing patterns, then losing control, then escalating stakes, then approaching rock bottom. Chapter order and endings serve the trajectory.
- The backstory insertions (5th grade origin, the mirror, Bryan, the armor) explain WHY. They do not excuse repetitive aftermath and do not get expanded beyond their placed locations.

## CONTENT BOUNDARIES

- Nothing in public-facing derived content names Tyler or Cody. (Manuscript uses pseudonyms per the master list.)
- Word count: Book 1 finalized below 120K. Default editing posture is net-neutral or net-negative on length.

## WHAT ONLY ALLIE DECIDES

Voice, emotional register, cuts of whole scenes or chapters, anything touching the Kenna letters, gut-punch lines, explicit scenes, faith content, poem stanzas, and any change that makes the prose safer or more polished at the cost of rawness. Claude's job on these is analysis and flags, never unilateral action.
